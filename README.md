# Dear Future Me

> *Write a letter. Pick a date. Send it to your future self.*

**Dear Future Me** is a cute little web experience for writing messages to your future self.
Instead of sending a letter to someone else, you write one for **you** — and choose when you want to open it again.

Maybe it's a message for next month.
Maybe it's a reminder of who you are today.
Maybe it's a tiny time capsule filled with dreams, thoughts, goals, or things you don't want to forget. ♡

---

## What It Does

**Write a Letter**
Write anything you'd like your future self to read.

**Choose a Date**
Pick the date when your letter should be opened.

**Seal Your Letter**
Once you're finished, your letter becomes a little digital time capsule.

**Future Countdown**
See how long remains until your letter can be opened.

**Reflect & Remember**
Come back later and see what your past self wanted you to remember.

---

## The Idea

Sometimes we forget how much we change.

The things we care about today might become memories tomorrow.
The problems that feel huge today might eventually become small.
And the dreams we have now might actually come true.

**Dear Future Me** gives those thoughts somewhere to live.

> *A tiny message from who you were
> to whoever you're becoming.*

## 🚀 Getting Started

```bash

```

```bash

```

Then open `index.html` in your browser.

No backend or installation required. ♡
